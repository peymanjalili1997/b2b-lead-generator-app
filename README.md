## ✨ Key Features

- **🎯 Precision Lead Discovery**: Extract verified local businesses by specifying any target niche keyword and location worldwide.
- **📱 Mobile vs. Landline Separation**:
  - Automatically identifies direct **Mobile phone numbers** (`09...`, international mobiles) for WhatsApp / Telegram / SMS campaigns.
  - Distinguishes landlines and searches business websites to extract hidden mobile numbers.
- **✉️ Automated Email Extraction**:
  - Deep-crawls company websites for `mailto:` contacts.
  - Multi-engine fallback web search to locate registered business emails.
  - 1-click `Mail` button and instant copy.
- **🔍 Social Profile Detection**:
  - Automatically uncovers verified profiles on **Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, and X (Twitter)**.
- **💬 1-Click WhatsApp Outreach**:
  - Instantly opens direct WhatsApp chats with leads without having to save contacts on your phone.
- **⭐ Permanent Favorites CRM**:
  - Star leads into your persistent favorites database across different searches and sessions.
  - Private CRM lead notes and pipeline status tracking (`New`, `Contacted`, `Interested`, `Closed`, `Rejected`).
- **📊 Export to CSV**:
  - Full data export with phone types, extracted emails, social URLs, pipeline stages, and notes.
